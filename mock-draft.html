Scoring System

  • 1 point — Player is selected in the first round
  • 3 points — Player drafted by the correct NFL team
  • 3 points — Correct position drafted by the team
  • 3 points — Player mocked to the correct draft slot

More accuracy = more points.

Example

In the 2026 draft where the Raiders held the #1 pick and selected Fernando Mendoza, QB here is how the scoring would be:

Raiders select Fernando Mendoza, QB - Correct player, at the correct slot, to the correct team. — 10 points

Raiders select Ty Simpson, QB - Team selecting the correct position, wrong player. — 4 points

Jets trade to #1 and select Mendoza, QB — Correct player selected in the correct slot. — 4 points

Raiders select David Bailey, EDGE — Correctly having him in the first round. — 1 point
